Procurement, as a controlled process

IT companies need a paper trail. We run RFQ to rack-in as a single owned workflow.

1. Spec

Workshops with infra and finance. We capture SKUs, warranty term, lead time, and who signs the PO.

  • BOM and alternatives
  • TCO vs cheapest quote
  • Compatibility with what you already run

2. Quote

Comparable quotes, not a single opaque number. Serial policy, RMA path, and delivery SLA in writing.

  • OEM / authorised channel
  • GST and freight called out
  • Validity dates you can plan to

3. Fulfil

PO tracking, warehouse check-in, serial capture, and site delivery with a named engineer.

  • Delivery challan vs PO
  • Dead-on-arrival process
  • Unpack and burn-in options

4. Support

Install, firmware baseline, warranty registration, then AMC or on-call hardware support.

  • Rack, cable, and handoff
  • Break-fix against SLA
  • Refresh and buy-back advice
