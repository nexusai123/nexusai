What we do

Services

NexusAI is a consulting and delivery partner for IT companies. We advise the work, procure the hardware, automate with AI, develop the software, and support it in production.

Consulting

We sit with your stakeholders, map the current system, and write a plan the business and engineering can both sign. No generic playbook — a scoped recommendation, risks, and a sequence of work.

  • Discovery workshops and current-state mapping
  • Architecture and operating-model advice
  • Roadmaps, estimates, and vendor choices
  • Reviews before you commit budget

Hardware procurement

IT firms should not assemble a datacenter or office fleet from scattered quotes. We specify the BOM, run procurement, and stay for install, warranty, and AMC.

  • Servers, storage, network, and endpoints
  • RFQ, comparable quotes, and PO tracking
  • OEM-grade supply with serial records
  • Break-fix and annual maintenance

Full hardware offering

AI automation solutions

We build automation that sits in the path of real work: incident triage, document-heavy ops, and repeat runbooks — with humans still in control of the decision.

  • Workflow automation around your tools
  • Assistive analysis for production incidents
  • Runbook and knowledge-base assistants
  • On-prem or private-cloud options

Development

From a signed design to working software: applications, integrations, and internal platforms. Short cycles, visible demos, and a handover your team can operate.

  • Web and backend application builds
  • APIs and system integrations
  • CI/CD so releases are repeatable
  • Docs, tests, and knowledge transfer

Production support

Go-live is not the finish. We cover incidents, health checks, and root-cause work so the system you just shipped stays usable on a Monday morning.

  • Agreed coverage windows and escalation
  • Incident handling and RCA
  • Monitoring health checks
  • Stabilisation after major releases
